Our Story

Rooted in community, built for the next generation

Youth for Positive Change started as a small circle of community volunteers in Tanzania, responding to a simple observation: talented, capable young people were being held back not by ability, but by access to mentors, to skills training, to a first real chance.

Today we run structured mentorship, vocational and digital skills programs, and youth-led community service across several regions, built on the belief that lasting change is led by young people themselves.

“We don’t just prepare youth for the future. We build the future with them, starting now.”YPC Youth Programs Team

Our Mission

To equip young Tanzanians aged 15–29 with mentorship, skills and leadership opportunities that open real paths to income, service and self-reliance.

Our Vision

A Tanzania where every young person has the access, support and confidence to shape their own future and their community’s.

What We Run

Four paths, one direction forward

Each program meets young people where they are and opens a door they can walk through on their own.

🤝

Mentorship & Life Skills

One-on-one and small-group mentorship pairing youth with community role models: confidence, decision-making and life planning.

💻

Vocational & Digital Skills

Practical training in trades and digital tools, from basic computing to entrepreneurship, aimed at real local income.

🌱

Community Service Corps

Youth-organized clean-ups, tutoring and elder support that build community trust and leadership experience.

🧭

Youth Leadership Circles

Peer-led groups where young people plan and run their own initiatives, with staff supporting rather than directing.
