Our Story

Rooted in community, built for the next generation

YOUTH FOR POSITIVE CHANGE started as a small circle of community volunteers in Tanzania, responding to a simple observation: talented, capable young people were being held back not by ability, but by access — to mentors, to skills training, to a first real chance.

The Youth for Positive Change initiative grew out of that work. Today we run structured mentorship, vocational and digital skills programs, and youth-led community service across several regions, built around the belief that lasting change is led by young people themselves, not delivered to them.

"We don't just prepare youth for the future. We build the future with them, starting now." — YOUTH FOR POSITIVE CHANGE, Youth Programs Team
What We Run

Four paths, one direction forward

Each program is designed to meet young people where they are — and open a door they can walk through on their own.

Mentorship & Life Skills

One-on-one and small-group mentorship pairing youth with community role models, covering confidence, decision-making and life planning.

Vocational & Digital Skills

Practical training in trades and digital tools — from basic computing to entrepreneurship — aimed at real, local income opportunities.

Community Service Corps

Youth-organized service projects — clean-ups, tutoring, elder support — that build both community trust and youth leadership experience.

Youth Leadership Circles

Peer-led groups where young people plan and run their own initiatives, with foundation staff supporting rather than directing.
